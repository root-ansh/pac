What are Prout Cooperatives?

A human-centred approach to progress, utilisation and balance

PROUT is a socio-economic philosophy that emphasises the rational use of available resources, local self-reliance and welfare for all.

01

Progressive Utilisation

Maximum and balanced use of material, human and natural resources.

02

Economic Democracy

Active participation of local people in their local economy.

03

Balanced Development

Harmony between the needs of individuals, society and nature.
